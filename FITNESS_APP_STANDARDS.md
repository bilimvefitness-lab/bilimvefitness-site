# FITNESS APP PRODUCT STANDARDS & EXPERT DOMAINS

This document defines the high-level product engineering principles and specialized domains for the entire application.

## 1. PRODUCT DECISION RULES
When multiple solutions are possible, we always prioritize:
1. **User Friction**: Reducing friction is the highest priority.
2. **Instant Feel**: The app must feel fast and responsive (momentum).
3. **Data Trust**: Protecting the integrity of user logs and calculations.
4. **Retention**: Improving consistency and daily return rates.
5. **Production Safety**: Preferring maintainable and safe solutions over "clever" ones.

**We Reject Solutions that**:
- Are "clever" but brittle or hard to maintain.
- Create any risk of silent failures.
- Depend on fragile text parsing when structured state is possible.
- Add UI complexity without a direct increase in user action.
- Make the app feel slower or reduce user trust.

## 2. SPECIALIZED FITNESS'DOMAINS
We maintain deep expertise in:
- **Nutrition**: Parsing, clarification, macro accuracy, and logging flows.
- **Workouts**: Logging, exercise libraries, and session tracking.
- **Biometrics**: Steps, hydration, sleep stages, and recovery scoring.
- **Gamification**: Streaks, challenges, and social motivation/leaderboards.
- **Visuals**: Progress visualization and premium mobile UX.
- **Optimization**: Onboarding for specific user goals (Fat Loss, Muscle Gain, Habit Formation).
- **Growth**: Mobile conversion thinking and retention loop design.
- **Coaching**: Feedback systems and AI-driven insights.

## 3. ENGINEERING'MANTRA
**Correctness > Reliability > Speed > UX > Polish.**
Never sacrifice the truth (data integrity) for appearance.
